# Glossary

Each term links to the page that owns its full definition. A term defined here in one line is defined there in full. This page never carries a rule of its own.

- attachment registry: the data directory's `projects/` tree. A plan is attached exactly when one slot holds its repository. [specs/SPEC-attachment.md](./specs/SPEC-attachment.md)
- blocked: a derived condition, never a lane and never a field. An entry is dependency-blocked through `needs` and question-blocked through a `Blocks:` line. [explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md](./explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md)
- burned id: an id held by a tombstone, for the life of the record. The mint refuses it, and a claimant is a validation failure. [specs/SPEC-ids.md](./specs/SPEC-ids.md)
- canonical YAML subset: the narrow YAML the record accepts. It allows flat mappings, one nesting level, and one-line flow sequences for lists. [specs/SPEC-lane-file.md](./specs/SPEC-lane-file.md)
- capture: writing a story document plus a pending fragment, without touching a lane file. [explanation/replication.md](./explanation/replication.md)
- chore: a work type whose outcome is upkeep. It needs no worked example. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- closure: this record and every peer it reaches, transitively. It is the set the checker proves the dependency graph over. [explanation/plans-and-peers.md](./explanation/plans-and-peers.md)
- cold reading: a watch observation whose `Read` date is older than its resolved cadence. [specs/SPEC-watches.md](./specs/SPEC-watches.md)
- current story: the head of the computed order for `doing.yml`, or for `todo.yml` when `doing` is empty. [specs/SPEC-reporting.md](./specs/SPEC-reporting.md)
- cycle time: first entry into `doing` until close, read from the journal. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- declared set: every row of this record's peer table, and of each of theirs, transitively. It is the set the attachment walk fills. [specs/SPEC-peers.md](./specs/SPEC-peers.md)
- drain: the act that lands pending fragments into lane files. [specs/SPEC-drain.md](./specs/SPEC-drain.md)
- drift: a fact a fragment states that the record has since outgrown. It is reported, never repaired. [specs/SPEC-pending-fragment.md](./specs/SPEC-pending-fragment.md)
- eligible: every id in `needs` is closed and no open question or open blocking watch blocks the entry. [explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md](./explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md)
- epic: an end state no single story delivers. It is one document plus an `epic` field on entries. [explanation/epics.md](./explanation/epics.md)
- external source: a system outside the plan repository holding items the project's work answers, declared once in the plan configuration's `sources` section under a local alias. [specs/SPEC-external-sources.md](./specs/SPEC-external-sources.md)
- exit condition: the testable condition that removes a watch after the outside action reaches the required result. [specs/SPEC-watches.md](./specs/SPEC-watches.md)
- fragment: one file under `pending/` stating a captured entry and its planning lane. [specs/SPEC-pending-fragment.md](./specs/SPEC-pending-fragment.md)
- host: the project whose identity the plan serves. It carries `.wipctl/project.toml` and nothing else of the tool's. [explanation/host-integration.md](./explanation/host-integration.md)
- id: an entry id when unqualified. The terms `project id` and `plan id` always name their distinct identities. [specs/SPEC-ids.md](./specs/SPEC-ids.md)
- initiative: an end state no single epic delivers. It is one document plus an `Initiative` section on the epics that serve it, and it is the ladder's third tier and its last. [explanation/initiatives.md](./explanation/initiatives.md)
- key chain: the ordered comparisons that compute a lane's rank from record facts and finish with the full id. [specs/SPEC-ranking.md](./specs/SPEC-ranking.md)
- lane: one of `backlog`, `todo`, `doing`, `review`, `closed`. The file records lane membership, and the key chain computes its order. [specs/SPEC-lane-file.md](./specs/SPEC-lane-file.md)
- mint: allocating an id by slugifying the title and checking it, under the lock, against the live record and every tombstone. [specs/SPEC-ids.md](./specs/SPEC-ids.md)
- needs: the ids an entry depends on. It is the only sequencing fact the record stores. [specs/SPEC-lane-file.md](./specs/SPEC-lane-file.md)
- peer: another project's plan repository, named by this plan and attached on this machine. Once attached it is a plan, with no distinction anywhere. [explanation/plans-and-peers.md](./explanation/plans-and-peers.md)
- peer alias: the local name this plan gives a peer. It is chosen by this plan, so two plans can use one alias for different peers. [specs/SPEC-peers.md](./specs/SPEC-peers.md)
- plan repository: the git repository holding a project's record. There is one per project, at a machine-level slot, shared by every checkout, hosted wherever its operator chooses. [specs/SPEC-zone-layout.md](./specs/SPEC-zone-layout.md)
- plan trunk: the plan repository's one published branch, where every mutation is a commit in the `plan:` grammar. [specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md](./specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md)
- plan configuration: the plan's one configuration file, `.wipctl/plan.toml` at the zone root. It restates the project id and carries every fact about the plan itself, including the peers it names and the sources it references. [specs/SPEC-configuration.md](./specs/SPEC-configuration.md)
- plan id: the plan repository's identity. It is 32 lowercase hexadecimal characters, minted once, immutable, opaque, and written into peer rows. [specs/SPEC-configuration.md](./specs/SPEC-configuration.md)
- plan zone: the plan repository's working tree, the directory holding the record. [specs/SPEC-zone-layout.md](./specs/SPEC-zone-layout.md)
- points: `1`, `2`, or `3`. They are units of irreducible human judgment, counting reviewer attention. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- prefixed id: `<alias>#<id>`, a `needs` id naming an entry in a peer. It means what a bare id means, and it is split exactly once. [specs/SPEC-lane-file.md](./specs/SPEC-lane-file.md)
- project id: the host project's identity. It is 32 lowercase hexadecimal characters, minted once, immutable, opaque, and committed by both repositories. [specs/SPEC-configuration.md](./specs/SPEC-configuration.md)
- project configuration: the project's own configuration file, `.wipctl/project.toml` at the project root. It carries the project id and nothing else. [specs/SPEC-configuration.md](./specs/SPEC-configuration.md)
- project root: the directory holding `.wipctl/project.toml`. [specs/SPEC-configuration.md](./specs/SPEC-configuration.md)
- project slug: the readable project name derived from the forge, the main worktree, or the project root. It displays a project and never selects a path. [specs/SPEC-slot-naming.md](./specs/SPEC-slot-naming.md)
- read set: every peer a prefixed id in this record reaches, transitively. It is the set validation requires attached, and it is a subset of the declared set. [specs/SPEC-peers.md](./specs/SPEC-peers.md)
- rank: an entry's computed position in its lane's key-chain order. [specs/SPEC-ranking.md](./specs/SPEC-ranking.md)
- rename: the operation that changes a title, an id, or both while holding them in agreement. It rewrites every reference and burns the old id, except where it frees nothing. [specs/SPEC-rename.md](./specs/SPEC-rename.md)
- resolve: recording a person's decision on a conflict the replication verb reported. [specs/SPEC-sync.md](./specs/SPEC-sync.md)
- rework rounds: entries into `doing` beyond the first. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- review dwell: time between entry into and exit from `review`. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- slugify: the one function that turns a title into its id's slug. It lowercases, removes apostrophes, turns every other non-alphanumeric run into a single hyphen, and trims the ends. [specs/SPEC-ids.md](./specs/SPEC-ids.md)
- source alias: the local name a plan repository gives one external source. It is chosen by this plan, so two plans can use one alias for different systems. [specs/SPEC-external-sources.md](./specs/SPEC-external-sources.md)
- source reference: one token, `<alias>#<key>`, naming an item in a declared external source. It is stated, searchable, and never verified. [specs/SPEC-external-sources.md](./specs/SPEC-external-sources.md)
- slot name: the local slug stored only as a directory name for one plan on one machine. [specs/SPEC-slot-naming.md](./specs/SPEC-slot-naming.md)
- spike: a work type whose outcome is a decision or a measurement. It exits through a recorded decision, a story revision, or a closed measurement. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- stale: an in-flight entry whose age is past the `stale_after` threshold the plan configuration declares. The ordinary stale view shows the row unless this machine suppresses its reminder. With no threshold declared, no row is stale. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- start: the atomic take. It reads the head of `todo`, moves it to `doing`, and prints it, in one transaction. `next` tells you, and `start` takes it. [specs/SPEC-transitions.md](./specs/SPEC-transitions.md)
- story: one vertical, demonstrable change, in one document. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- suppression: local state that hides one stale reminder from the ordinary stale view and its warning count, for a stated period or until an operator removes it. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- sync: replication between machines. It fetches, reconciles semantically, validates, and pushes fast-forward. [specs/SPEC-sync.md](./specs/SPEC-sync.md)
- task: a checklist line inside a story, never a record of its own. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- the travelling document: the zone's `AGENTS.md`, which carries the complete method and is the single source an agent reads through `wipctl man`. [specs/SPEC-scaffold.md](./specs/SPEC-scaffold.md)
- tombstone: a journal whose last event targets `deleted` or `renamed`. It is the surviving trace of an entry that left the record. [specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md](./specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md)
- transaction lock: the exclusive per-project, per-machine lock a writer holds from the deciding read to the final commit, and never for a session. [specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md](./specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md)
- transition event: one lane change. It carries an instant, the lane left, and the lane entered. [specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md](./specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md)
- transition journal: one append-only stream per entry recording its transitions. [specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md](./specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md)
- velocity: points of accepted, closed work per window, derived on every read. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- window: the stretch of time every count is totalled inside, fixed by an anchor date and a length in days. The plan configuration declares both. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- workflow verb: a verb that operates the record. `help`, `version`, and `man` operate the program instead. [specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md](./specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md)
- warning: a diagnostic that reaches the reader and never the exit code. The reader decides its resolution, and the drain handles a drift as stated. [specs/SPEC-validation.md](./specs/SPEC-validation.md)
- watch: an outside item or actor that blocks named entries until a person confirms its exit condition. [specs/SPEC-watches.md](./specs/SPEC-watches.md)
