# Glossary

Each term links to the page that owns its full definition. A term defined here in one line is defined there in full. This page never carries a rule of its own.

- attachment registry: the data directory's `projects/` tree. An id is attached exactly when its slot holds a plan repository. [specs/SPEC-attachment.md](./specs/SPEC-attachment.md)
- blocked: a derived condition, never a lane and never a field. An entry is dependency-blocked through `needs` and question-blocked through a `Blocks:` line. [explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md](./explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md)
- burned id: an id held by a tombstone, for the life of the record. The mint refuses it, and a claimant is a validation failure. [specs/SPEC-ids.md](./specs/SPEC-ids.md)
- canonical YAML subset: the narrow YAML the record accepts. It allows flat mappings, one nesting level, and one-line flow sequences for lists. [specs/SPEC-lane-file.md](./specs/SPEC-lane-file.md)
- capture: writing a story document plus a pending fragment, without touching a lane file. [explanation/concurrency.md](./explanation/concurrency.md)
- chore: a work type whose outcome is upkeep. It needs no worked example. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- current story: the topmost entry of `doing.yml`, or of `todo.yml` when `doing` is empty. [specs/SPEC-reporting.md](./specs/SPEC-reporting.md)
- cycle time: first entry into `doing` until close, read from the journal. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- drain: the act that lands pending fragments into lane files. [specs/SPEC-drain.md](./specs/SPEC-drain.md)
- drift: a fact a fragment states that the record has since outgrown. It is reported, never repaired. [specs/SPEC-pending-fragment.md](./specs/SPEC-pending-fragment.md)
- eligible: every id in `needs` is closed and no open question blocks the entry. [explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md](./explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md)
- epic: an end state no single story delivers. It is one document plus an `epic` field on entries. [explanation/epics.md](./explanation/epics.md)
- fragment: one file under `pending/` stating where a captured entry wants to land. [specs/SPEC-pending-fragment.md](./specs/SPEC-pending-fragment.md)
- host: the project whose identity the plan serves. It carries `.wipctl.toml` and nothing else of the tool's. [explanation/host-integration.md](./explanation/host-integration.md)
- id: the title's slug, plus at most a qualifying postfix where that slug was taken. One id names one thing, and no consumer parses its interior. [specs/SPEC-ids.md](./specs/SPEC-ids.md)
- initiative: an end state no single epic delivers. It is one document plus an `Initiative` section on the epics that serve it, and it is the ladder's third tier and its last. [explanation/initiatives.md](./explanation/initiatives.md)
- lane: one of `backlog`, `todo`, `doing`, `review`, `closed`. The file is the lane, and the position is the ranking. [specs/SPEC-lane-file.md](./specs/SPEC-lane-file.md)
- legal ranking: an order satisfying R1 and R2. [explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md](./explanation/lanes-and-ranking.md)
- mint: allocating an id by slugifying the title and checking it, under the lock, against the live record and every tombstone. [specs/SPEC-ids.md](./specs/SPEC-ids.md)
- needs: the ids an entry depends on. It is the only sequencing fact the record stores. [specs/SPEC-lane-file.md](./specs/SPEC-lane-file.md)
- plan repository: the git repository holding a project's record. There is one per project, at a machine-level slot, shared by every checkout, hosted wherever its operator chooses. [specs/SPEC-zone-layout.md](./specs/SPEC-zone-layout.md)
- plan trunk: the plan repository's one published branch, where every mutation is a commit in the `plan:` grammar. [specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md](./specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md)
- plan zone: the plan repository's working tree, the directory holding the record. [specs/SPEC-zone-layout.md](./specs/SPEC-zone-layout.md)
- points: `1`, `2`, or `3`. They are units of irreducible human judgment, counting reviewer attention. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- project id: the project's identity. It is a minted slug, committed in the host's `.wipctl.toml`, restated in the plan repository, and the key everything resolves through. [specs/SPEC-attachment.md](./specs/SPEC-attachment.md)
- project root: the directory holding `.wipctl.toml`. [specs/SPEC-configuration.md](./specs/SPEC-configuration.md)
- rename: the operation that changes a title, an id, or both while holding them in agreement. It rewrites every reference and burns the old id, except where it frees nothing. [specs/SPEC-rename.md](./specs/SPEC-rename.md)
- resolve: recording a person's decision on a conflict the replication verb reported. [specs/SPEC-sync.md](./specs/SPEC-sync.md)
- rework rounds: entries into `doing` beyond the first. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- review dwell: time between entry into and exit from `review`. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- slugify: the one function that turns a title into its id's slug. It lowercases, removes apostrophes, turns every other non-alphanumeric run into a single hyphen, and trims the ends. [specs/SPEC-ids.md](./specs/SPEC-ids.md)
- spike: a work type whose outcome is a decision or a measurement. It exits through a recorded decision, a story revision, or a closed measurement. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- start: the atomic take. It reads the head of `todo`, moves it to `doing`, and prints it, in one transaction. `next` tells you, and `start` takes it. [specs/SPEC-transitions.md](./specs/SPEC-transitions.md)
- story: one vertical, demonstrable change, in one document. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- sync: replication between machines. It fetches, reconciles semantically, validates, and pushes fast-forward. [specs/SPEC-sync.md](./specs/SPEC-sync.md)
- task: a checklist line inside a story, never a record of its own. [explanation/stories-and-estimation.md](./explanation/stories-and-estimation.md)
- tombstone: a journal whose last event targets `deleted` or `renamed`. It is the surviving trace of an entry that left the record. [specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md](./specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md)
- transaction lock: the exclusive per-project, per-machine lock a writer holds from the deciding read to the final commit, and never for a session. [specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md](./specs/SPEC-cli-conventions.md)
- transition event: one lane change. It carries an instant, the lane left, and the lane entered. [specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md](./specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md)
- transition journal: one append-only stream per entry recording its transitions. [specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md](./specs/SPEC-transition-journal.md)
- velocity: points of accepted, closed work per iteration window, derived on every read. [specs/SPEC-metrics.md](./specs/SPEC-metrics.md)
- warning: a diagnostic that reaches the reader and never the exit code. The repair can resolve one on request, and the drain handles a drift as stated. [specs/SPEC-validation.md](./specs/SPEC-validation.md)
